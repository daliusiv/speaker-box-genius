Extremely stiff and rigid aluminium cone gives tremendous bass precision.
The cone and the long throw low loss rubber surround show no sign of the familiar
cone edge resonance and distortion associated with soft cones.
Lead-out wires symetrically stitched to the spider to avoid resonances. Total
suspension designed to assure stability for extreme excursions.
2-layer, extremely long, high temperature voice coil wound on a glass fiber voice
coil former gives a high power handling capacity.
Cu-cap around the pole pieces reduce non linear and modulation distortion and
increase overload margin.
Extra large magnet system provides high efficiency and low Q.
