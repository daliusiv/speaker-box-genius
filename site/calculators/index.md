## Which one do I need?

- **You have a sub and want to know what box to build:** the [subwoofer box](/calculators/subwoofer-box/) calculator gives a compact, a balanced and a deep box, sealed or ported.
- **You're sizing a sealed box:** the [sealed box calculator](/calculators/sealed-box/) gives the volume for the Qtc you want, or the Qtc, F3 and cone excursion of the box you have.
- **You want a ported box for your driver:** the [ported box](/calculators/ported-box/) calculator: QB3, C4 and BB4 boxes with a port that fits and its air speed.
- **You know the box you'll build, or the litres you need:** the [box volume calculator](/calculators/box-volume/) gives the net volume after the walls, driver, port and bracing, for any shape, or the size for a net volume.
- **You have a box and want to tune it:** the [port length](/calculators/port-length/) or [passive radiator](/calculators/passive-radiator/) calculator.
- **You want the whole picture** (response, cone excursion, port speed, maximum SPL, group delay): [open the simulator](/).
