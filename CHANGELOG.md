# Changelog

What's new in Speaker Box Genius. Each numbered section below is one update of the live site: a new minor version (1.1.0) adds features, a new patch (1.0.1) has only changes and fixes. The dated sections are from before 1.0.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

### Changed
- Driver and passive radiator database: the search box, the Filters button and the header's + and × are all one height (40 px on touch screens, with 16 px text so phones don't zoom in), and the brand list and chips match the rest of the app. The filters fold away behind **Filters** on desktop too, so the list starts right under the search box; the button shows how many filters are on. On phones each driver is one two-line row (name, then size · Ω · Fs · Qts · Xmax) instead of a table cut off at the edge, and **Sort by** is the first choice under Filters.
- Driver name: renaming moved from the database search to the top of **Edit T/S** as **Name**. A driver from the database shows its name as fixed text ("· from database"); change one of its T/S values and the name becomes yours to edit, with "(edited)" added, so shared links and exports don't pass it off as the datasheet. Submit to database leaves "(edited)" out.
- Versions: each update of the live site is now a numbered version, starting with this one, 1.0.0. The menu shows it beside Changelog.
- The "Early preview" label is gone from under the app's name.
- Guides: the steps name the current tabs, cards and rows (Settings → Simulation / Limits, the Driver card's Wiring and Compare rows, the Preset list, the Build tab's Box card), and the screenshots of the settings, the T/S editor, the Build tab and the response chart show the current app.
- Driver tab: a calmer driver card. The picker shows the model, brand, impedance and size on one line (the search box says how many drivers there are), with Specs ↗ and Edit T/S beside the title and the number of drivers as a − 1 + stepper. The rest sits in rows that show their values while closed: Large signal (Xmax, Pe), Coil & suspension (Le, coil and loss models as cards), Wiring (loading, voice coils and wiring as switches, Znom, and the amplifier's nominal and lowest load) and Compare. Edit T/S opens the T/S parameters under the picker, or full screen on phones, with Fill from (paste a datasheet, measure from a .zma, a WinISD or OpenISD file), Submit to database and Clear (with Undo); units sit inside the boxes. It opens by itself for a driver you type in, and a note says when values are missing. Exporting a driver as WinISD .wdr is in Save & Export.
- Driver card: every control is the same height and width, 32 px with 13 px text with a mouse and 40 px on touch screens (taps still land across 44 px), so number boxes no longer look larger than the switches beside them. The compare picker matches the driver picker. Loading reads Normal / Isobaric.
- Boxes tab: **Preset** opens a list like **+ Box** instead of a plain drop-down. Each preset shows the box it gives for this driver (volume, tuning and F3), a line on what it is, and a small curve of its response from the same simulation as the charts; the current one is ticked. Changing Vb or the port by hand still makes it read Custom.
- Help: explanations no longer pop up while you type or move the mouse. An open row with settings to explain shows an ⓘ beside its chevron that opens what each of its settings does (including the ones that only appear after a choice, and option cards with the program each matches); settings outside rows have one ⓘ in their card's title row, and a box card's ⋯ menu has **About these fields**. On phones it opens full screen. The dotted underlines on labels are gone.
- Settings tab: three cards instead of one long form. **General** holds Units and **Match** (Default, WinISD, Hornresp or VituixCAD as option cards, or "Custom: WinISD except Ports"). **Simulation** holds the conventions Match sets, as rows that show their values while closed: Amplifier & cable, Air, Voice coil, Box (presets and losses), Ports and SPL. Each modelling choice is an option card with a line on what it does and the program it matches. **Limits** holds what describes your build: the amplifier's volts and amps (with **From the amp's ratings**), the chuffing line, Peak / RMS and PR caution, and the air distortion limit. The "About" paragraphs became one short line each; the full text is in its help.
- Build tab: two calmer cards. **Box** (the volume calculator) starts with Used by, then rows that show their values while closed: **Shape** as option cards with a line on each, **Outside size** ("40 × 50 × 45 cm · 18 mm", with the walls and the 3D view; a car wedge's fields, its side view and an **Angles and inside** row), **Driver fit** for a car wedge, **Inside parts** ("−3.9 L"), **Net volume** with its breakdown, and **Size for a volume** with **Size box**. **Cut list** shows the summary and parts at once; **Holes** holds the cutouts with the hole list and **Sheet layout** the sheet size and kerf with the layout. Print, Copy cut list and FreeCAD macro are in one **Export** menu (Print is **PDF**); the printout is unchanged, with every row open.
- Signal tab: three cards instead of six. **Signal** holds High-pass, EQ and Passive parts in the order the signal meets them; **Listening space** holds Room / cabin gain and Baffle step; **Export to DSP** is last. Each setting is a row that shows its value while closed ("Butterworth 12 dB/oct · 18 Hz", "2 filters", "Car cabin · from 69 Hz"). EQ filters are rows that show their values, with On first; **+ Filter** adds one (or clears them). Passive parts has an on switch, **Fill from** for the starting networks, branches as rows, and R, L and C marked with their part symbols. Space and Baffle are option cards with a line on each choice; **Compensate** holds the +3 / +6 dB shelves. The long explanations moved into each setting's help, and the DSP formats read Equalizer APO, CamillaDSP 3, miniDSP and CSV coefficients.
- Boxes tab: calmer box cards. The top row is **+ Box** (the box types, each icon in the colour its curves will get), **Size all** and one button that folds or opens every box. Each card's head has the name over its type and preset (or, folded, its volume, tuning, F3 and limits), the show-on-charts eye and a ⋯ menu (Duplicate, Size from dimensions, Unlink, Delete). Inside, **Preset** comes first as a choice, then Vb and Fb; the port, the passive radiators, a bandpass box's chambers and losses, and stuffing open as rows that show their values while closed, with units inside the boxes. A preset reads Custom once you change the box by hand. Bandpass types read 4th order, 6th parallel and 6th series, and **Size for a flat band** is now **Size this bandpass**.
- Numbers in every field and readout use tabular figures, so digits line up in columns and values don't shift width as they change.

### Fixed
- Power and voltage slider: the value beside it keeps one width, so the slider no longer shrinks and jumps under your finger as the number gets longer (most visible on phones).
- Phones: the summary's over-limit badge reads "⚠ 3 over ›" and its text is a little smaller, so it fits beside the box count and F3 range instead of covering them.
- Phones: the legend table under each chart is more compact (rows about 36 px instead of 53 px). Rows that fold open still take taps across 44 px.
- Phones: the Power bar sits between the response chart and the other charts instead of staying pinned above them, so it no longer takes screen height while you scroll. Desktop keeps it pinned above the charts.
- Phones: a chart's switches (Relative / dB SPL, SPL / Volts / Amps, Ω / Phase) sit on the title row, sized to their labels, instead of a full-width row of their own: about 30 px less height per chart. They still take taps across 44 px.
- Boxes: the Preset list opened with ↓ shows each preset's curve and F3, and starts on the ticked preset. The curves leave out passive parts too, so they compare the boxes alone.
- Help: an ⓘ no longer opens an empty sheet (Net volume, Cut list), and a setting with option cards is listed once (Coil model, Suspension losses).
- Phones: Units, Peak / RMS, Round / Slot and the other side-by-side choices are 40 px tall on touch screens, steppers and the box name are as tall as the fields, and "How wiring works" and "Fit to a measured impedance sweep" take taps across 44 px.
- Imperial units: the PR finder gives the target box in ft³ (it said litres), and preset volumes show two decimals.
- Phones: scrolling a chart's legend table sideways (Air distortion and other charts with extra columns) no longer clears the cursor, so the cursor column and the chart's own columns stay while you scroll to them.
- Validation docs: the circuit (SPICE) check covers 441 compared cases (plus the free-air driver, checked against closed forms); the text said 439. A test now keeps the text in step with the published results.
- Passive radiators whose maker publishes only the mechanical limit (27 PRs: SB Acoustics and Purifi) now say so: the database keeps the figure as Xmech (`xmech_mm`) instead of Xmax, the PR field and the PR excursion chart's redline read "PR Xmech" (in the app and on the driver pages), the passive radiator calculator labels the field Xmech, the PR list marks them M, and the "new passive radiator" issue form takes an Xmech when there is no Xmax. The simulation is unchanged: it still checks against that figure, which is where the PR bottoms out, not a linear limit.
- Response chart edit mode: dragging a handle (a box's tuning, an EQ point, the high-pass) no longer pushes the chart down. The line with the dragged value is always there in edit mode and stays on one line (a long box name is cut short), so the plot stays still under your finger.
- Foldables and tablets with the browser's address bar showing: the driver and passive radiator database, the design assistant and the ☰ menu fit the visible screen, so the database's title, + and × are no longer cut off at the top.

### Changed
- Stuffing → Fit to a measured impedance sweep now says how far to trust the result: "Likely 43.0–55.4 L and Qa 12–27", from refitting with the driver's Vas, Fs, Qes and Qms each off by its tolerance plus how flat the fit is (combined root-sum-square), and which of them moves it most. A database driver's untouched datasheet values get production tolerances (Vas ±25 %, Fs ±15 %, Qes ±15 %, Qms ±20 %); your own values Vas ±10 %, Fs ±2 %, Qes ±5 %, Qms ±10 %. A wide range (volume ±15 % or Qa over 2.5×) adds a note to measure the driver first. The Fit button reads "Fitting…" while it runs.
- Driver pages are listed in one sitemap per brand (sitemap-drivers-<brand>.xml), so Search Console shows how many of each brand's pages are indexed.
- Sitemap dates only move when a page's visible content changes: the app state in share links is ignored, and the dates are restored from the live site if the build cache is gone.

### Added
- Driver pages: one page per driver size (10-inch, 12-inch, 6.5-inch and so on, for sizes with ten or more drivers) lists every driver of that size by kind, sorted by Fs, with its suggested ported box. They are linked from the driver list, brand pages, and each driver's opening line and similar drivers, which now always show at least four (up to six from the same brand, then the same size and type from other brands, closest Fs first).
- Driver pages: a driver sold in several impedances or voice-coil versions (18 Sound's 4, 8 and 16 Ω, Dayton's -4 and -8, Purifi's M04 and M08) links its other versions with "Also available" buttons, and its opening line says what differs: Re, sensitivity at 2.83 V and the suggested box size. The versions no longer appear in each other's similar drivers.

## 2026-11-13

### Added
- Edit mode on the response chart (✎ Edit): tap the chart to place a cursor, then "+ Add" opens a list (Parametric EQ, Low shelf, High shelf, High-pass, Linkwitz Transform for sealed boxes) and adds the filter at the tapped frequency, starting at 0 dB (peak Q 1.4) so nothing changes until you drag it. The high-pass shows as a ▲ handle on its corner (−3 dB, or −6 dB for Linkwitz-Riley): drag it (5–200 Hz) or press Delete on it to turn it off. A dashed curve shows the EQ chain on its own (high-pass included) for the box being edited, and the other boxes fade. Filter chips under the chart pick a filter (a dot shows which box it applies to), opening a small panel under the chart: a bin to delete it, a type menu to switch it (say, from a peak to the high-pass), one row of fields (Freq, Gain and Q; fp and Qp for a Linkwitz Transform; frequency and slope for the high-pass), ✓ to keep the filter as it is and ✕ to put the EQ and high-pass back as they were when you picked it. A Limits strip under the plot marks frequencies near (80 %+) or past Xmax, PR Xmax, port speed or air distortion for the boxes being edited, with a line naming the box and limit; when a filter pushes a box past a limit, the panel says which limit and offers the gentlest fix as a button ("Cut to +4.7 dB", or "Raise fp to …" for a Linkwitz Transform). While editing, the chart and its controls sit in one tinted panel that ends at Cancel and Done; everything stays under the plot, the chips scroll sideways and the buttons are finger-sized on phones.
- Settings → SPL → "F3 from": Passband (like WinISD, the default) or Response peak (like VituixCAD). Passband takes the −3/−6 dB points from a plain cone's mass-controlled level; peak takes them from the response's highest point in the bass (searched up to 5 × the box's Fc or Fb; if it's still rising there, the passband is used). A box with a hump or a high Q shows the difference: a vented box with a 1.3 dB hump reads 27.7 Hz from the peak, 26.6 Hz from the passband. The relative response chart, its −3/−6 dB lines, the F3 dots, the room and car F3 and measured-curve matching follow the choice, so in peak mode the hump's top sits at 0 dB. The summary's details now show F6 and F10 too. Checked against VituixCAD 3's Enclosure tool (validation page).
- Ported and passive radiator boxes: the port or PRs can sit on the back panel (the volume calculator's depth) or a set distance from the cone, and a parallel 6th-order bandpass box's two openings can be apart. In front of the box the far one's sound arrives later (VituixCAD hears the port from its far end, which explains its slightly lower group delay); from radiated power it works as Hornresp's Acoustic Path Length (checked against Hornresp: within 0.015 dB). The Response chart shows the same box with the port next to the cone, dashed. LTspice netlists include the delay; WinISD exports note that it's left out.
- Settings → Match has a fourth preset, VituixCAD, beside Default, WinISD and Hornresp. It sets F3 from the response peak, coil inductance off (VituixCAD's enclosure tool leaves it out of the SPL), leaked air lost, absorption that also makes the box bigger, and port sound from the port's far end (passive radiator boxes too, by the spacing of the driver's and the PR's centres); air is standard (VituixCAD's is within 0.4 %), and losses, Rg and units are kept. With it, VituixCAD 3's Enclosure tool and the app agree within 0.3 Hz on f3, f6 and f10, about 0.15 ms on group delay and about 1 % on impedance peaks. Two new settings do the work and can be used on their own: Settings → Box losses → "Absorption" (damping only, the default, or damping + bigger box, which makes the box act as Vb × (1 + 0.9·Qa^−0.9): +1.4 % at Qa 100, +11.3 % at Qa 10; ignored when stuffing is set) and Settings → Ports → "Port sound" (where each box puts it, the default, or from the port's far end, which delays a ported box's port by the port's length, and a passive radiator by the side-by-side spacing of the centres). Bandpass boxes have a leak between the chambers (Qicl 100 by default); VituixCAD has none, so clear Qicl to compare. Alignment presets, bandpass chambers and WinISD/LTspice exports follow the bigger box.
- Hornresp records for sealed, ported and passive radiator boxes: Save & Export → Hornresp record writes them (lines and horns too), with a port's distance from the cone as the Acoustic Path Length, and Open reads a Hornresp record as a new box or line with its driver.

### Changed
- Cancel in chart editing also restores the high-pass; new chart EQ points start at 0 dB.
- Phones: the drive bar above the charts is one row, Power, slider and value. Tap the value to type a power or voltage, switch watts/volts, or pick 1 W or the rated power in a sheet from the bottom of the screen. Wider screens keep the full bar.
- Impulse and step charts follow Settings → SPL for a line's mouth distance (and a box's port position): in front of the box the far opening's delay now shows there too.
- Lines and horns: with SPL from radiated power, a mouth further away than the cone (on the back, or a set distance) now works as Hornresp's Acoustic Path Length, so the two programs agree. Hornresp exports carry the distance as the Path, and imports read it back.
- Lines and horns: in front of the box, a mouth further away than the cone is now heard later at the same level, as a box's port is (a far listener). It used to be made quieter by the extra distance too, which showed bass below the line's tuning that isn't there from further away. Lines with the mouth on the back or a set distance read a little different in the deep bass and above.

### Changed
- Charts on desktop: the hover box now shows each design's peak and every column the table under the chart had (Limited by, Absolute…), so that table is gone on desktop and the charts take less height. Phones keep the table. In the Relative response view, the Absolute column is now "vs loudest": each box's level against the loudest box at that frequency (0.0 dB for the loudest), since the relative curves hide which is louder; the dB SPL view still shows the levels.
- Passive radiator boxes: the box's settings (presets, volume, PRs, added mass, tuning) sit in their own "Box" panel, like the passive radiator's panel under them.

### Fixed
- Best tuning (passive radiators), Smallest box: "Reach F3 of" now starts at the design's F3 as it is now (shown next to it), so it finds the smallest box that keeps the bass you have. It used to start 3 Hz deeper, which nearly always suggested a bigger box, and kept an old target when the window was opened again.
- Desktop side panel: the last box can be reached by scrolling the panel alone, without first scrolling the page.
- "+ Add a box": the menu fits on screen with every box folded.
- Spacing and alignment: the Port choice no longer touches a ported box's presets; "Size for a flat band" sits clear of a bandpass box's volumes; a bandpass box's Type, Opening and Port choices line up; Settings → Ports lines its choices up and pairs End correction K with Chuffing line; no divider line before the Settings tab.
- Phones: Settings fields and menus are now 44 px tall, easier to tap.
- Chart legends: the fold-out arrow next to a design with parts (a line's cone and mouth) was drawn huge over its name.
- Validation page: the Hornresp box cases set up in its wizards (bandpass, high-pass, EQ, stuffed lines) are labelled Hornresp 60.60, the version they were run on, not 60.70.

## 2026-11-12

### Added
- Air distortion: a new "Air distortion" chart shows the 2nd harmonic a box's air adds when a big cone squeezes a small box (sealed, ported, passive radiator and bandpass boxes), in % or dBc, at the power set or at each box's max SPL, with each box's peak above the chart. The summary gets a "Peak air" column, red over your limit (10 % by default, Settings → Air distortion). It can also count as a Max SPL limit (off by default, so Max SPL stays as WinISD shows it), and the CSV export carries it. Box fill (empty to packed) lowers it a little. It's the box's air only: the driver's own distortion isn't modelled.
- Air distortion now covers transmission lines, MLTLs, tapped and front-loaded horns too: a strong wave steepens as it travels down a line or horn, adding 2nd harmonic that grows with pressure, path length and frequency. Shown on the same chart (at the mouth, over the whole sweep), in the summary's "Peak air" column and, if counted, in Max SPL. Checked against Thuras, Jenkins and O'Neil's 1935 tube and horn measurements (validation page).
- Open / Import window (☰ → Open / Import…): your three latest browser saves (and the rest a click away), and one place to open project, WinISD, OpenISD and measurement files. Drop a file anywhere on the page to open it; a .zma asks whether to measure the driver from it or show it on the charts.
- Save & Export window (☰ → Save / Export…): every export in one place, picked from a list: browser save, project file, CSV, WinISD project or driver, LTspice netlist, DSP filters. Tick the boxes to include; several files come as one zip. Driver tools → Export WinISD .wdr opens it too.
- Validation against Hornresp re-run on Hornresp 60.70 (identical results) and widened to 73 cases: twelve more lines and horns (other flares, three-segment lines, offset and tapered lines, tapped and front horns with two segments, two drivers in a line), fifteen more boxes (two passive radiators, added mass, lossy radiators, a small PR box, a 12″ subwoofer and a 4″ full-range sealed and ported, every coil model at once, two drivers in series, full space), port and mouth air speed, and maximum SPL with the amplifier's current limited.
- This changelog, at /changelog/ and linked from the site footer: what changed in each daily update.
- The validation page has a new section: every box type, filter and EQ checked against an independently built equivalent circuit solved by a circuit simulator, across 436 cases, with the netlists to download.
- Volume calculator → Car wedge (single slope): the box that sits behind a car's rear seat, with its exact inside volume (the sloped panel is wider across than it is thick), a double-baffle option, slope on the front or the back, butt-and-bevel or mitred corners with their saw angles, a live side-view drawing, Solve for any one dimension, checks with one-tap fixes, and whether the driver's motor clears the back and bottom. The cut list builds it too, with the bevels to cut. "Truncated pyramid" is now called "Tapered (all sides)"; older links still open.
- Build → Cut list → FreeCAD macro: download the box or car wedge as a FreeCAD script that builds every panel as its own solid (bevels, miters and the baffle's driver, passive radiator and port holes included) plus the air inside, and checks that volume against the app's when it runs. Cylinders, spheres and tapered boxes come out as the wall and the air inside.
- Volume calculator → 3D view: a 3D model of the box, car wedge, tube, sphere or tapered box, with the driver, port and passive radiator holes. Drag (or use the arrow keys) to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom, jump to the 3/4, side or top view, and slide **Take apart** to pull the panels off one by one, with the inside shown as a ghost. Hover or tap a panel for its name and the size to cut it. Box panels are the ones the cut list gives. Passive radiators and ports that don't fit beside the driver go on the back (a tapered box also tries its sides), and anything that doesn't fit where it's drawn is named under the view.
- Purifi passive radiators now carry Purifi's maximum added mass (1.5× the PR's own moving mass), so the passive radiator calculator warns when a tuning needs more.
- Save & Export → LTspice netlist: download any box (sealed, ported, passive radiator or bandpass, with its filters, EQ and passive parts) as an LTspice circuit, with notes on what to plot.
- Cross-checks against REW (DSP filters, T/S fitting from impedance files), XSim (passive parts), FreeCAD (box volumes) and WinISD (slot port length), described in the validation notes.

### Fixed
- Guides: curve colours named in five picture captions (port chuffing, port tuning, Linkwitz transform, passive radiator, transmission line) now match the chart, a few numbers rounded by 0.1 match the app again, and the port chuffing example's slot now gets its own colour.
- Forum image: the response and cone excursion charts were drawn as narrow strips when they were taller than the other charts on screen (a narrow window or a phone); each chart now fills its panel at its own shape, and the chart title no longer shows the on-screen ▾.
- The menu's Roadmap link pointed to a page that no longer exists; it now opens the "What's next" list.
- Hornresp import: tapped horns with four segments now read the front tap where Hornresp has it (where the last segment starts); the export says so when a horn has more than one section after its tap.
- Hornresp export: the semi-inductance and frequency-dependent damping models are switched on in the record itself, so Hornresp opens them on, with no double-clicking; the import leaves those values out when a record has the model switched off, as Hornresp does.

### Changed
- Guides: four more figures are drawn when the site is built: the amplifier's power into each load with the subs' impedance ranges, sealed box size against Qts, the error of two tapered-box volume shortcuts, and what 0.15 Ω of amplifier and cable costs each DVC wiring.
- Guides: the figures worked out from the simulation but not drawn by the app (one 15″ minus two 10″, cabin gain by cabin length, box + cabin, the baffle step alone and by driver position, watts at a set voltage, ports as pipes) are now drawn when the site is built, with the numbers their captions quote. A few move slightly: the 15″/10″ curves cross at 42 Hz (was 43), one sub draws 299 W at 25 Hz (was 293), the free-standing bookshelf loses 5.9 dB of maximum output at 50 Hz (was 5.8), and the port pipe peaks are 2.3 and 4.2 dB (were 2.4 and 4.3).
- Guides: the side-by-side and multi-curve figures in the "Box stuffing", "Power compression", "Hofmann's Iron Law", "Sealed, ported or PR", "How to compare two drivers", "Round or slot port", "DVC wiring" and "Matching an amplifier" guides are now the app's own charts, drawn when the site is built, with each chart labelled; on phones they stack. Their curve colours follow the app (the compare-drivers figure's F3s had drifted by 0.1 Hz from its caption).
- Guides: the numbers in the "Compare two drivers", "Sealed, ported or PR", "Passive radiator box", "Thiele/Small parameters", "One 15 or two 10s", "Group delay", "Linkwitz Transform", "Isobaric", "Box stuffing", "Box losses", "Port chuffing", "Subsonic filter", "Port tuning", "Transmission line", "Simulation vs measurement", "Design assistant" and "Matching an amplifier" guides now come from the simulation when the site is built; a few move by 0.1–0.3 dB (and the PR box's amplifier voltage at 25 Hz to 32.2 V), because they're now read at exactly the frequency the text gives. The Linkwitz Transform guide's V²/Re watts now use the same Re as the app's drive setting (326 W and 179 W, from 349 W and 191 W), and its group delay figures now include the example's 18 Hz high-pass filter. The box stuffing guide's sealed chart now names its curves' colours correctly. The simulation vs measurement guide's baffle step loss reads 5.8 dB (was 5.7). The amplifier guide said the parallel pair loses 0.7 dB at the tuning in one place and 0.3 dB in another; it's 0.3 dB.
- Guides: the picture shown when a guide is shared (on Reddit, diyAudio, Discord…) is now drawn from its chart with the guide's title, so it stays up to date too. The group delay guide's step response chart is drawn from the app as well.
- Guides: the charts in 18 more guides are now the app's own charts, drawn when the site is built (impedance and phase, port speed, passive radiator travel, group delay, line load, amplifier volts and current, and the rest), so they keep up with the app.
- Guides: the car subwoofer guide's charts are the app's own charts, drawn when the site is built, and its numbers and curve colours come from the same simulation, so they can't fall behind the app. More guides will follow.
- Chart legends: until you hover or tap the chart, the table shows just each curve and its peak (the columns read at the cursor appear when there is one), and long names wrap to two lines instead of being cut off.
- New look: a deeper navy background with lifted cards, cyan for everything you can tap or toggle (the logo stays orange), and the Inter typeface for crisper labels and numbers. Chart colours are unchanged. The app icons and share images match.
- Chart tabs sit on one row that scrolls sideways (with a fade where there are more), the picked chart underlined in cyan. Panel tabs, switches and chips are quieter: the selected one is tinted cyan, the rest grey. On phones the bottom bar has icons, and a chart's switches get a row of their own under its title.
- Notes about a chart (what it includes, where its settings are) moved behind an (i) button beside its title, starting with Air distortion and Max SPL.
- Charts: hover or tap and the frequency (or time) shows in a cyan tag at the top of a dashed cursor line, with a dot on every curve where it crosses. Units sit at the plot's corners (the value's above the scale, Hz beside the first frequency), phones included.
- Every chart has a legend table under it, on computers and phones alike: each box with its value at the cursor, its peak (and the frequency, in small type under it) and the chart's own columns, such as Air squeezed and Max SPL set by on Air distortion, or what limits each box on Max SPL. A line's cone and mouth fold under it (tap ▾). The hover box on computers is laid out the same way. Air distortion's peaks line above the chart moved into the table.
- The ☰ menu is shorter and never scrolls: New project, Open / Import…, Save / Export…, the site's pages, the app, and a footer of links, with icons. Ctrl/⌘+S opens Save & Export at "Save in this browser" (Enter saves); Ctrl/⌘+O opens Open / Import.
- Share opens a window instead of a menu: the live link with a Copy button, a preview of the forum image (download it or copy it straight to the clipboard), Reddit and diyAudio post text, and on phones the system share sheet. File exports (CSV, WinISD, LTspice) moved to Save & Export.
- WinISD projects: a slot port is now written as WinISD's own rectangular vent (it was a round port of the same area), and a rectangular vent in a WinISD project opens as a slot.
- The port's pipe resonance now says it's worked out from the length with end corrections, and gives WinISD's "1st port resonance" figure (physical length only, so higher) beside it, in the app and the port length calculator.
- Project files are now saved as .sbgenius instead of .json, so they're easy to tell apart. Older .json project files still open, and so does a project renamed to .txt. On a computer, the installed app opens .sbgenius files with a double-click.
