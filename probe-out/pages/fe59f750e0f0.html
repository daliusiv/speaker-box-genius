We are pleased to introduce the M64F001 (E0103-06), a classic fabric dome midrange driver engineered for the highest standards of audio reproduction.

The fabric dome midrange is a timeless design used extensively in professional studio monitors and high-fidelity speaker systems worldwide. Now, SEAS has refined this proven architecture with modern materials and precision engineering to deliver exceptional clarity and musicality.

At the heart of the M64F001 is a custom-manufactured fabric dome with a special coating. This lightweight material ensures high sensitivity throughout the working frequency range while providing smooth roll-off characteristics at both ends of the spectrum, making it ideal for seamless integration with other drivers in multi-way systems.

The M64F001 delivers exceptional power handling capabilities, rated at 150 W long-term and 350 W short-term. This robust performance stems from its 2.5-inch Copper Clad Aluminium voice coil wound on a ventilated aluminium former, together with a large motor featuring a neodymium ring magnet and a well-vented pole piece. The overhung voice coil configuration ensures sufficient linear excursion margin, allowing the driver to handle demanding passages without compression or distortion. These elements converge to create a midrange capable of sustaining high output levels while maintaining the clarity and detail that SEAS Excel drivers are known for, whether in professional studio monitoring or high-fidelity home systems.

Don't just listen to your speakers, experience the warmth and detail that only a classic fabric dome midrange can deliver. Prepare to be captivated by the M64F001, where tradition meets innovation in high-fidelity audio.
