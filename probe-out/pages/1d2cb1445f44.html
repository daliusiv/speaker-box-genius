Introducing SEAS Storeglimt — a new high-performance loudspeaker kit

We are proud to introduce SEAS Storeglimt, a new reference-level loudspeaker kit combining the finest SEAS Excel driver technology with a classic wide-baffle three-way design.

Designed for those seeking uncompromising musical performance, Storeglimt delivers a powerful yet refined listening experience with exceptional dynamics, natural tonality, and outstanding detail. The carefully selected Excel drivers work together seamlessly to create an open and immersive soundstage, from deep, controlled bass to effortless and precise high frequencies.

The name Storeglimt reflects its Norwegian heritage: store means "big," representing the speaker’s generous proportions and wide-baffle design, while Glimt pays tribute to the exceptional SEAS tweeter that completes the system.

With Storeglimt, SEAS continues its tradition of creating high-performance DIY loudspeaker kits that bring world-class sound and the satisfaction of craftsmanship into the home.

SEAS M64F001 Fabric Dome Midrange

We are pleased to introduce the M64F001 (E0103-06), a classic fabric dome midrange driver engineered for the highest standards of audio reproduction.

The fabric dome midrange is a timeless design used extensively in professional studio monitors and high-fidelity speaker systems worldwide. Now, SEAS has refined this proven architecture with modern materials and precision engineering to deliver exceptional clarity and musicality.

At the heart of the M64F001 is a custom-manufactured fabric dome with a special coating. This lightweight material ensures high sensitivity throughout the working frequency range while providing smooth roll-off characteristics at both ends of the spectrum, making it ideal for seamless integration with other drivers in multi-way systems.

The M64F001 delivers exceptional power handling capabilities, rated at 150 W long-term and 350 W short-term. This robust performance stems from its 2.5-inch Copper Clad Aluminium voice coil wound on a ventilated aluminium former, together with a large motor featuring a neodymium ring magnet and a well-vented pole piece. The overhung voice coil configuration ensures sufficient linear excursion margin, allowing the driver to handle demanding passages without compression or distortion. These elements converge to create a midrange capable of sustaining high output levels while maintaining the clarity and detail that SEAS Excel drivers are known for, whether in professional studio monitoring or high-fidelity home systems.

Don't just listen to your speakers, experience the warmth and detail that only a classic fabric dome midrange can deliver. Prepare to be captivated by the M64F001, where tradition meets innovation in high-fidelity audio.

Glass Tweeter Release

We are thrilled to announce the release of our first tweeter with a glass dome, the T27GL001-DXT (E0102-06).

Glass has fascinated audio engineers for some time, and now it is finally possible to create an ultra-thin tweeter glass dome with an optimal weight to stiffness ratio. This allows for precise control of breakup behaviour while taking full advantage of the fast sound propagation inherent in the glass material. The result is a tweeter with lightning-fast transients, revealing every detail in music with realism. Glass is naturally weatherproof and performs superbly in even the dampest conditions.

The T27GL001-DXT faceplate uses our DXT® technology for optimum directivity control, a favourite amongst HI-FI enthusiasts as well as professional audio engineers. For the T27GL001-DXT, we have upgraded the DXT® concept with an ultra-stable machined aluminium faceplate.

Experience a new dimension in high fidelity tweeters with the new T27GL001-DXT, with clarity and detail that only SEAS Excel tweeters can provide.

New DIY kit: SEAS 403 Revisited!

We are proud to introduce the SEAS 403 Revisited – a modern reinterpretation of one of our classic loudspeaker designs, the SEAS KIT 403 from the 1970s.

This project brings together decades of audio craftsmanship, modern engineering, and a passion for timeless sound. Developed in collaboration with cultural entrepreneur and music lover Martin Revheim, the 403 Revisited pays tribute to SEAS’ 75-year legacy of innovation while making the joy of true high-fidelity audio more accessible than ever.

The kit is a classic wide-baffle, 3-way design housed in a beautiful standmount enclosure. It delivers a smooth and balanced sound with impressive sensitivity and a rich, controlled bass response from its 10-inch woofer. The speaker’s natural tonality and musicality make it equally at home in casual listening spaces and dedicated hi-fi setups – a timeless design reimagined for modern music lovers.
